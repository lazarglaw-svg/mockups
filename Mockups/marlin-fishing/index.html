Good to know

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions most guests have before booking a fishing charter in Cancún.

Do I need fishing experience?+

No. The crew can guide complete beginners through the setup, technique and what to do once a fish is on.

Which trip length should I choose?+

Four hours is a good introduction, six hours gives you more offshore time, and eight hours is best when fishing is the main focus of your day.

What happens if the weather is bad?+

Safety comes first. If conditions are not suitable, the operator should discuss rescheduling or the applicable weather policy with you.

Can children join the trip?+

Many private charters welcome families. The best trip length depends on the child’s age, sea conditions and comfort on the water.

Are there any extra marina or conservation fees?+

Some marinas or trips may have separate local fees. Any additional charges should be confirmed clearly before you book.

Will we definitely catch fish?+

No charter can guarantee a catch. The captain chooses the area and technique based on the season, conditions and what has been biting recently.
